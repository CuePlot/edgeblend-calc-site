EdgeBlend Calc Support

EdgeBlend Calc helps projection professionals plan screen dimensions, projector grids, overlap areas, brightness levels, viewing distance, and lens shift positioning.

Contact

For support requests, bug reports, or feature questions, contact: erdemakdag@hotmail.com

Before contacting support

  • Include the app version shown in the top-right corner of the application.
  • Describe your screen size, projector count, resolution, and lens settings.
  • If possible, include a screenshot of the issue.

Project files

EdgeBlend Calc project files are stored locally on your Mac when you choose to save them. The app does not require an online account.
