Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Overview

EdgeBlend Calc is designed as a local projection planning tool. The app does not require account registration and does not collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or user tracking.

Data stored on your device

Project data that you create or save in EdgeBlend Calc is stored locally on your Mac in files that you choose. The app does not automatically upload your project files to a server.

Internet access

EdgeBlend Calc may use an internet connection when you use projector lookup features or other online reference features. This access is used to retrieve projector-related reference information requested by the user.

Personal information

EdgeBlend Calc does not sell, share, or use personal information for advertising. If you contact support by email, your email address and message content will be used only to respond to your support request.

Third-party services

Online lookup features may connect to third-party reference websites or data sources when requested by the user. Those services may have their own privacy practices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: erdemakdag@hotmail.com
